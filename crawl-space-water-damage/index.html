Emergency Crawl Space Water Damage Cleanup in DeSoto, TX

Water under a house is easy to miss. Soft floors, a musty smell and sagging insulation are the usual clues, and a crawl space needs its own tools to drain and dry.

How common crawl spaces are in DeSoto and what we see

A fair warning first. Many newer North Texas homes sit on concrete slabs, so crawl spaces are the less common foundation here. Raised pier and beam foundations appear mostly under older houses, some additions and porch areas. If your home is on a slab, the slab leak water damage page is the better fit.

If you do have a crawl space, water enters in a few ways. A supply or drain line under the floor can leak. Rain can run toward the foundation and drain through a vent or a gap. Regional extension-based guidance describes the clay soil around Dallas and Fort Worth as swelling when wet, which can slow drainage after heavy rain.

Local weather shows what is possible. In August 2022, local news reports citing the city counted at least 19 homes with water inside after torrential rain overwhelmed drainage in parts of DeSoto. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows your flood zone, and you can ask the city about drainage near your lot.

First-hour safety steps for a flooded crawl space

Stay out

A wet crawl space is a confined space with electrical, structural and sometimes animal hazards. Do not crawl in alone.

Power off

Turn off power to circuits that run under the house if you can reach the panel on dry ground.

Stop the source

If a supply line is leaking, close the main valve. If it is rain, note where it is entering.

Call for help

Call (972) 945-2885. Crews use small pumps and protective gear for tight spaces.

How crews clean up a crawl space

Crews begin by checking the space for safety, electrical hazards and the source of water. Standing water is pumped out with small submersible pumps and hoses run to a safe discharge point. Where the soil is soaked, crews work on plastic sheeting to stay out of the mud.

Wet insulation hanging under the floor is removed. It holds water against the joists and subfloor and does not dry well. Joists and subfloor are checked with a moisture meter. Dehumidifiers and air movers are set up, often with ducting from outside the space, and readings are logged until the wood meets the dry standard under ANSI/IICRC S500.

Crawl spaces can hold mold. When growth is present, the work changes and Texas licensing rules apply, as the mold remediation page explains. Fixing drainage, grading or vapor barriers is separate contractor work.

What changes the cost of crawl space cleanup

  • Depth and area. More water over a larger area takes longer to pump
  • Access. Low clearance and small doors slow the work
  • Insulation. Wet insulation under the floor is removed and replaced
  • Water source. Clean supply water and storm water are handled differently
  • Soil conditions. Soaked soil dries slowly
  • Mold. Growth on joists or subfloor adds a separate scope

Insurance and crawl space water

Most home policies leave out flood damage, according to the Texas Department of Insurance, and flood cover is a separate policy. Water from a broken line under the house is a plumbing loss and may be treated differently. Read your policy, and note that most flood policies have a 30-day waiting period.

Photograph the space and the water level before pumping if you can do so safely from the access door. Make only temporary repairs until the insurer has seen it.

Mistakes to avoid with crawl space water

  • Closing the vents and hoping. Trapped moisture feeds mold
  • Entering alone. Confined spaces and wiring are dangerous
  • Pulling insulation yourself. It is heavy when wet and can hide wiring
  • Ignoring a musty smell. It usually means moisture is present
  • Skipping drying after pumping. Wet wood stays wet
