Red Oak: a small city on the Ellis County side of the line

Red Oak is in northern Ellis County and sits about 18 miles south of downtown Dallas via Interstate 35E, which has two exits in the city. The 2020 census counted 14,222 people, and the city incorporated in 1949. It was once called Possum Trot and took the Red Oak name in 1849.

Its neighbors include Glenn Heights, Lancaster, Ferris, Pecan Hill, Waxahachie and Oak Leaf. Because the ZIP code 75154 carries the mailing names Glenn Heights, Oak Leaf and Ovilla as well, a Red Oak mailing address does not always mean a Red Oak city address.

That is worth checking when you report a loss or ask for a permit. If your street address is in Red Oak, you work with the City of Red Oak. If it is in Glenn Heights or Ovilla, you work with those cities and potentially Dallas or Ellis County records.
