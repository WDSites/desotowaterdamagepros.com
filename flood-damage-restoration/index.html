Emergency Flood Damage Restoration in DeSoto, TX

Flood water is contaminated and moves fast. Stay out of it, call the 24/7 line once you are safe, and a crew will extract, clean and dry the structure.

When flood water is an emergency

Flood damage is an emergency from the first inch. Water that came from outside is contaminated, it can carry electrical risk, and it soaks into materials faster than most people expect. The 24/7 line is open, and the dispatcher can help you decide what to do first.

While you wait, keep everyone out of the water. Do not use electrical appliances in wet areas, avoid contact with the water and call 911 if you smell gas or see sparks. Take photos before moving anything, because your insurer will want to see the damage.

Get out first

If water is rising or moving, move to higher ground. Do not walk or drive through moving water.

Power and gas

Leave power alone if water reached the panel or outlets. Call 911 and your utility for gas smells or sparks before you call the restoration line.

Document

Photograph the water line on the walls and the damaged contents once it is safe to enter.

Call your insurer

Report the loss promptly and ask what your policy covers. Flood is usually a separate policy.

What flood damage restoration involves

Flood damage restoration deals with water that is almost always contaminated. The job runs through extraction, removal of porous materials, cleaning and sanitizing, drying and then rebuild. It follows the same steps as other water losses, but with protective gear and stricter material decisions.

Crews cut drywall above the water line, remove carpet, pad and insulation, and clean the framing. The structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers, and readings are logged. The water damage cleanup page covers what is kept and what is replaced.

Basement flooding cleanup

If your home has a basement, a walkout level or a below-grade room, flood water collects there first. Basements are also harder to dry, because concrete holds water and outside soil can keep feeding moisture through the walls.

The crew pumps out the water, removes wet drywall and carpet and checks for moisture in the concrete and framing. Drying a basement can take longer than a wood-framed floor. If water keeps coming in after rain, the cause is drainage or the foundation, and a specialist should look at it.

Do not pump a flooded basement too fast. Water outside the walls pushes against them, and pumping the inside dry all at once can stress the foundation. The crew pumps down in stages and checks conditions as it goes.

Toilet or floor drain backing up? Sewage backup cleanup

A toilet, tub or floor drain pushing dark, foul-smelling water into the house is a sewage backup. It is a contamination event, so stay out of the water, keep children and pets away, do not run water or flush, and call the 24/7 line. Crews extract the water, remove porous materials, disinfect and dry. The sewage backup cleanup page walks through the process and how to tell whether the blockage is on the city side.

DeSoto rain, drainage and flood insurance

Local conditions matter. In August 2022, local news reported that the city counted at least 19 homes with water inside after torrential rain overwhelmed drainage in parts of DeSoto, including the Wolf Creek Estates area. The city has also been working on a streambank erosion program funded from its drainage fund, which shows drainage is an active concern.

Find your flood zone on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask the city about drainage near your lot. If your property sits in a designated flood zone and has a federally backed mortgage, your lender may require flood coverage. Even outside a zone, a flood policy is something to ask your agent about, since most flood policies have a 30-day waiting period before they take effect.

Crawl spaces and whole-house flooding

A whole-house flood means water reached the main living floor, so crews extract, strip wet materials to the water line, dry the structure and document the loss for the insurer. Standing water under the floor is handled differently, with small pumps and removal of wet insulation. See crawl space water damage for that case.

What affects the cost of flood damage restoration

  • Water category. Flood water is contaminated, so removal and sanitizing are part of the scope.
  • Depth and area. Higher water lines mean more drywall and contents affected.
  • Materials. Carpet, pad and insulation are usually removed, and hardwood and concrete need special drying.
  • Drying time. Basements and concrete often take longer.
  • Rebuild. Most flood jobs end in a significant rebuild scope.

Do you handle flood damage emergencies at night or on weekends in DeSoto?

Yes. Flooding often arrives with storms in the middle of the night, and the line is open 24/7. The dispatcher asks about depth, source and safety, then sends crews as availability allows.

If the floodwater is moving, deep or near electrical service, stay out and call 911 if anyone is in danger. Call the restoration line once everyone is safe.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage in Texas?

According to the Texas Department of Insurance, most home policies do not cover damage caused by floods. Flood coverage is a separate policy, and most flood policies have a 30-day waiting period.

Check your policy and ask your agent what you have. If you have a flood policy, call your insurer early.

How do I check whether my address is in a flood zone?

Use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and enter your address. The map shows the designated flood zone for your property, though flooding can happen outside designated zones.

The Texas Department of Insurance notes that more than half of homes flooded by Hurricane Harvey were outside designated flood zones, so a low-risk label is no promise of safety.

What is the difference between flood water and sewage backup?

Floodwater comes from outside, from storm runoff, creeks or overland flow. Sewage backup comes up through drains and toilets when a line is blocked or overwhelmed.

Both are contaminated and both need protective gear. Insurance treatment can differ, so ask your insurer how each is covered.

Can a basement or crawl space be dried after it floods?

Yes, when the water is removed and the structure is dried quickly. Standing water is pumped out, wet materials are removed and equipment dries the framing and concrete.

Water that keeps entering is a drainage problem. Restoration handles the damage, and a drainage or foundation specialist handles the cause.
