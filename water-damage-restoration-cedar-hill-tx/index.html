Cedar Hill neighborhoods and what the terrain does to water

Cedar Hill is about 16 miles southwest of downtown Dallas and sits on the eastern shore of Joe Pool Lake, a lake impounded in 1986 that covers 6,469 acres. The city rests on an Austin chalk limestone uplift at about 800 feet, which is well above DeSoto at roughly 666 feet.

That height difference changes the pattern of damage. Runoff runs off the ridge toward lower neighborhoods, while the western edge, including the Lake Ridge area, falls below the uplift onto Eagle Ford shale. If your lot sits at the bottom of a slope, check where the grading sends rain.

The City Center is split into Historic Downtown, Uptown and Midtown, and the shopping areas Cedar Village and Hillside Village run along US 67. Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center and Cedar Hill State Park, which opened in 1991 on 1,826 acres at the northeast corner of the lake, add large open land where heavy rain drains toward creeks and the lake.

Stop the source

Power and gas

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel dry. Leave and call 911 for a gas smell or sparks.

Lift and photograph

Move valuables above the water line and take photos before anything is disturbed.

Cedar Hill city contacts and permits

The City of Cedar Hill main number is 972-291-5100, and city staff can tell you about permit rules for plumbing, electrical and structural repairs. A leak inside the house is for you or your plumber to stop, and a suspected main break in the street is a call to the city.

Before you rebuild over a replaced pipe or rewired outlet, ask whether the work needs a permit and an inspection. Our crews can document the loss for your insurer, but the permit decision belongs to the city.

Does the limestone ridge make homes in Cedar Hill drain differently?

Cedar Hill sits on an uplift of Austin chalk limestone that rises to about 800 feet, so rain tends to run off the ridge toward lower ground. Homes at the foot of a slope can receive runoff from above, while homes on top of it may drain away from the foundation.

Check where your downspouts and grading send water. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center and the city can tell you about drainage near your lot.

I live near Joe Pool Lake. Do I need flood insurance?

Ask your insurance agent. Most home policies do not cover flood, and most flood policies have a 30-day waiting period. A designated flood zone can require flood coverage from your lender.

Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, but remember that flooding can occur outside designated zones.
