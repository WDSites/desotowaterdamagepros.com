Where Summer Meadow is

Summer Meadow is a residential community in DeSoto. A neighborhood directory says it dates to 2002 and lists homes built between 2004 and 2016, in sizes from about 1,900 to 4,200 square feet, both detached and attached. Directories also list Mockingbird Hill as a nearby neighborhood.

The details above come from directory data that we have not independently verified, and we could not find an official boundary.

Summer Meadow in the city drainage plan

The City of DeSoto's 2026 Drainage Master Plan names Summer Meadow among the five areas where engineers developed conceptual drainage solutions and lists a Summer Meadow storm sewer project. The plan does not report flooded homes in this community.

How water reaches homes of this age

Across DeSoto, the National Weather Service in Fort Worth reports that spring is the peak for severe weather, that large hail falls nearly every year and that summer highs are in the 90s and often above 100. Hail can open a roof, and a long summer keeps an air conditioner running.

That makes two sources worth knowing. An air conditioner drain line that clogs can overflow in a closet, attic or ceiling. A roof leak can show up as a stain far from the damaged shingles. Read AC leak water damage repair and roof leak water damage for what to check.

Multi-level homes and hidden water

In any multi-level home, water from an upstairs fixture can run down inside walls and show up in rooms below. Our kitchen water damage and wall and drywall water damage pages explain how crews find and open the wet areas.

Safety steps while you wait

Stop the source

Shut off the AC, the fixture or the main valve if you can reach it safely.

Watch the ceiling

A bulging ceiling can hold a lot of water. Keep people out from under it and do not poke it.

Photograph

Take photos of the stain, the source and any dripping before anything is moved.

Questions from Summer Meadow homeowners

Why is Summer Meadow in the city drainage study?

It is one of five areas the 2026 plan chose for conceptual storm sewer work. The document does not say homes there have flooded.

Is my ceiling stain from the roof or the AC?

A stain near the AC closet or under an attic unit often points to the drain line. A stain that grows after rain suggests the roof. A professional moisture reading can tell which.

Are the homes in Summer Meadow slab or pier and beam?

We have not verified the foundation type for the community, so we do not assume one. Tell us what you know about your home when you call.

Is Summer Meadow in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center with your address.

Should I turn off the AC when it leaks?

Yes, if you can do so safely. Turning off the system stops more condensate from entering the pan or ceiling while you wait for help.
