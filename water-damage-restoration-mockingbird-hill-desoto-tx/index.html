Where Mockingbird Hill is

Mockingbird Hill is a residential area in DeSoto's 75115 ZIP code. Neighborhood directories list Summer Meadow, Fern Heights and Park Ridge as nearby neighborhoods. We have not found an official boundary, so this is an approximate description.

Directories describe a mix of home types in the area, and we have not verified the mix. If your home shares a wall with another, tell us when you call, because leaks can cross between units.

The Mockingbird Hill storm sewer project

The City of DeSoto's 2026 Drainage Master Plan names Mockingbird Hill among the five areas where the city's engineers developed conceptual solutions, and it lists a Mockingbird Hill storm sewer project. The plan describes undersized pipes and limited overland relief as common problems in the system across the city.

It does not report flooded homes here. It also states that the city has not historically received frequent flooding complaints. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

Rain water, storm drain water and sewer water are different

Storm sewers carry rain from streets and yards. Sanitary sewers carry waste from toilets, sinks and drains. When water backs up through a floor drain or toilet, treat it as sewage, even after a rainstorm.

Rain water that has run across a street or yard is also not clean. Our sewage backup cleanup page explains what removal involves when waste is present. Do not use household fans, a wet vacuum or a mop to deal with it yourself.

Safety before cleanup

Keep people and pets out

Keep children and pets away from standing water and wet floors until the source is known.

Power off, carefully

Switch off power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.

Do not flush or run drains

If water is backing up, stop using toilets, sinks and washing machines until the line is cleared.

Removing the water and drying the structure

Crews extract standing water, remove materials that cannot be cleaned and dry what can be saved with air movers and dehumidifiers. The flood damage restoration and emergency water removal pages describe the steps. Moisture readings confirm the structure is dry before rebuilding begins.

Insurance questions

Coverage depends on the cause and the policy. The Texas Department of Insurance notes that policies often treat sudden plumbing events differently from flood or slow seepage. Photos, readings and a written scope can help describe the work. We cannot promise what your insurer will pay.

Questions from Mockingbird Hill homeowners

Why is Mockingbird Hill named in the city drainage study?

City engineers picked it for a storm sewer project in the 2026 plan. That is a planning item, and the plan does not report flooded homes in this area.

Is Mockingbird Hill in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. A search by address on FEMA's map site is the way to check one home.

How do I tell sewage from rain water?

Sewage usually smells strong, appears dirty or comes up through floor drains and toilets. If you are unsure, treat the water as contaminated and call before you handle it.

Can I clean it myself with a wet vacuum?

Not safely when the water may contain sewage or came from outside. Ordinary fans and vacuums can spread contamination and are not enough for contaminated water.

What if the leak came from a neighbor's unit?

Tell us when you call. Document the damage, notify the owner or property manager and check your own policy for what it covers.
